# Unit 7 — 35 Soal Teks

## Soal 1 — K03-ENG-B07-M01-001

**Pertanyaan:** The classroom is behind the office. Where is the classroom?

**Pilihan jawaban:**
- behind the office
- beside the office
- in front of the office
- between two offices

**Kunci Jawaban:** behind the office

**Pembahasan:** Kalimat menyatakan bahwa ruang kelas berada **behind the office** atau di belakang kantor.
