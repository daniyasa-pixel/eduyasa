## Soal 1 — K03-ENG-B04-M01-001

**Pertanyaan:** Which sentence tells your own hobby?

**Pilihan jawaban:**
- He likes swimming.
- I like swimming.
- Do you like swimming?

**Kunci Jawaban:** I like swimming.

**Pembahasan:** Use I like to tell an activity you enjoy.
